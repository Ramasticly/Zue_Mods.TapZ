## 11. Screens and buttons (what each control does)

### 11.0 Title bar
`↻` reloads the folders the app reads (asks for confirmation; unsaved edits are lost; keeps the same character, opponent and move when the folders still exist) — use it after the user adds, deletes or replaces a character folder or a `.txt` file. `PT` / `EN` / `ES` switches the app language instantly. `AJUDA` opens the in-app help (chapters, search, and the button that creates this file).

### 11.1 Top menu (16 buttons; the user can long-press and drag them to reorder; the first is pinned)
- `SALVAR` — save (section 13).
- `CHAR` — choose the **attacker** and the **opponent** characters; `POS` sets distance/height between them by dragging on screen (plus `ESQ.` / `MEIO` / `DIR.` to take the pair to the left edge / middle / right edge of the map, changing only x) (and `FLY`: attacker airborne); `CHAR OBJ` shows body box and sprite anchors and edits the body size. The +1 tick counter locks during hitstop and the cinematic lock and then continues at locked+1 (frozen ticks do not count). The ANO/move selector is a button that opens a grid. A `GOLPES | ANOS` switch at the top changes mode on the spot. The grid shows the first Frame of each item; tapping a card expands it and plays the animation without opening (landing simulation for aerial moves; loop for ANOs with wActFlag 1, others play once, hold the last Frame 1 s and restart; the hit/projectile box is drawn); the big `➜ ABRIR` button opens it for editing. In ANO mode: `FILTRAR` cycles Padrão / Importância / Golpes / Ataque / Tempo / Frames / Efeitos (reorders without hiding: matches go first with a green frame); in GOLPES mode the bottom row is `VER FLUXO` (opens the move's flow view-only), `COPIAR`, `COLAR` and `TROCAR`: `COLAR` pastes the copied move over the selected one (same character; 0, 3, 6 only over their own number) — its ANOs become NEW ANOs at the end of the DAC, the Bin row is renumbered, equal reactions are reused, otherwise they go to empty slots and with none free the user picks which to replace; `TROCAR` swaps the Bin rows of two moves only (the move number — and so the command that triggers it — changes; 0, 3, 6 only among themselves); both only change memory until SAVE (new ANOs are appended to 000.dac.txt). In ANOS mode at the bottom `＋ CRIAR` (new ANO = last+1, one frame IMG -1 with 6 ticks), `EXCLUIR` (empties the ANO, it keeps existing; warns if it has a hitbox/projectile or belongs to a BIN attack), `COPIAR`, `MOVER` (pick another ANO; the Frames go there and the original becomes empty) and `COLAR` (same warning); holding a card starts selection mode to delete several. The `ⓘ` button: on an ANO only its role (`ANIM_INICIAL do Golpe N`, `SEQ do golpe N`, `Projétil do golpe N`, `Animação de pouso padrão`, fixed game ANOs such as `Parado` or `Carga de ki`; ANOs 29–39 are the normal A attacks and 30/32 have the OPT 512 combo window); on a move, the button that triggers it with its icon from effect.pac plus a `Sequências:` block. Colors: blue = fixed in the game's source code, green = from the BIN or another source. Move block: green = BIN (`SEQ (chão/ar)`, `END (chão/ar)`, `SEQ PROJ` = `ANIM_AO_ACERTAR`, grab `OPONENTE_AGARRADO`); blue with `(automática)` = code (special repeat ANO 42/40-41 for moves 0/3/6 with `MAX_REPETICOES`, grab → `ANIM_INICIAL + 1`); landing is not listed. ANOs 29–39 show their continuation: ANO 30 → A:32, A+forward:31, A+back:37, A+down:36; ANO 32 → A:33 and the same directions; the others `Sem continuação`. Game buttons (`CreateGamepad` + `Controller`; pad key = 6th `AddPad` argument): A (effect.pac ANO 209) = normal attacks, fixed ANOs 29–39, not in HDAT 4 (ground: A neutral 30, forward 31, back 37, down 36, from a run 29; jumping: 34/35/39/38; combo window OPT 512 in ANOs 30/32: 30→32→33, or 31/36/37 with a direction); Y (330) = moves 0/3/6; X (333) = 1/4/7; B (336) = 2/5/8 (ground / jumping-falling / flying); Z (339, 100 Synchro = 1 ki bar) = moves 9 (ground/flying) and 10 (jump); IRA (ANO 45, no button of its own: tap of the corner button while `AngryOn`, 200 Synchro) = moves 11 (ground/flying) and 12 (air); the same tap without `AngryOn` and Angry >= 100 = Rage = moves 14/15. Y, X, B and Z exist only in the game's button layout (`iControlType == 1`); otherwise the specials come from a joystick gesture **followed by the A button** (the game only reads the gesture on the tick A is pressed, `JoyTrig & 670056704`): Y `↓ → + A`, X `→ ↓ + A`, B `↓ ← + A`, Z `↓ ← → + A`, IRA `↓ ← ↓ ← + A` (with `AngryOn`). Special moves 9-12 have the game's cinematic lock: the +1 tick button shows a yellow notice with a `PULAR` (skip) button that passes through the lock and releases the tick, and the camera zooms to 200% (4 ticks, centered on the owner: 40 px outward, 48 px above the feet) and back to 100% in 12 ticks after it. The attacker/opponent pickers are dark grids (3 columns) where each `charNN` plays its own ANO 76 (ANO 0 if it has none); the current one is highlighted. The opponent row is `OPONENTE: charNN | DEF`: the `DEF` checkbox (off by default) makes the opponent block, to test how the move behaves when blocked: no damage or reaction, guard stance (ANO 4, or 81 in the air), pushback 13 decaying 2 per tick, the OPT 1 hitstop, block effect ANO 2 and SE 8 (groups 0-3), 2 chip damage only when the hitting ANO is >= 40 and damage is not 0; hitbox flag 26 (enemy does not block) and grabs are not blocked, and a blocked hit still confirms the move (ANIM_SEQ/ANIM_END happen). With the opponent's `DEF` box on, a guarded hit does **not** confirm the hit (no SEQ / `ANIM_AO_ACERTAR` / landing-on-hit; those only happen when the move connects). Effects placed on the opponent's own frames (hit flags 71–125 → his character's ANO 201+, 10–17 effect, 41–70 charf; e.g. the visual of the defense on ANO 4) are created and drawn too.
- `SPRITES` — the character's sprite list: add, replace (the old sprite's pixels are erased from the sheet BEFORE the new one is placed, except what another sprite still uses, so a smaller sprite can take the same spot and the rest stays free and clean), remove (deleting takes the sprite out of the CNV AND ERASES its crop from the sheet — pixels become transparent even if another sprite uses the area — and deletes any PNG sheet left with no sprite from the folder, freeing the space for new sprites; on opening the list (a `Organizando as folhas` loading, once per session) everything outside the sprites' crops is erased from the sheets, the free spaces of all PNGs are read and every sprite that fits in a space of an EARLIER sheet is moved there (the first that fits, so the early sheets fill up and the last ones empty and leave the folder; the sprite number and the DAC do not change; a new sheet is still at most 1024×1024, only a sprite that exceeds that by itself gets a larger one; sprites whose crop partly overlaps another or has a crop error are not moved), and on every save the same erasing is repeated; it RENUMBERS the following sprites: SPR_0 out → 1 becomes 0, and the CNV, GIF groups and every DAC frame follow; removal goes through a decision screen per marked sprite (SPR_N + preview) — `Sprite, usar outra Sprite no lugar` (a list with a `Semelhança mínima` bar: 0% shows all sprites, raising it up to 90% keeps only the similar ones, each with its score; every IMG of the old sprite becomes the chosen one's and only then the sprites are renumbered, so IMG 0 → 40 ends as 39), `Sprite e Frames` (deletes the sprite and its frames; warns about hitbox/hurtbox/projectile and Bin or fixed-game ANOs) or `Sprite, Frames ficam sem imagem` (IMG -1) — plus the box `Alinhar MID/RAZ` (only with "use another": recalculates the frames' MID, rotation, zoom and flips so the new sprite looks like the old one, same logic as `Remover Sprites repetidas`); every choice opens an explanation screen and nothing changes until the final confirmation; the sprite tap menu has `Escolher do Personagem` (inside `Substituir`): the same IMG swap (same similarity list) WITHOUT deleting or renumbering; only the IMG changes, so a different size is warned about), reorder (hold and drag the `–` handle), change the cut, import PNG/JPG/GIF; also choose the sprite for a frame. Its `OTIMIZAR` button (next to `+ ADICIONAR`) has three functions: `Remover Sprites repetidas` (groups equal/similar sprites, also mirrored, at another scale or size, flattened (zoom X different from Y) or rotated by 90° or by ANY angle (the any-angle search runs once on opening with a progress bar and is approximate, shown with `~`); each row states the relation and warns when the kept sprite is smaller and must be enlarged; a minimum-similarity bar; nothing comes marked; tap a sprite of the group (not the largest, which stays as the reference) to open the comparison: ONE sprite that alternates every 400 ms (default; a chip changes it) the kept sprite, drawn with the zoom/rotation/flip adjustments to look like the compared one, and the compared one, with a caption like `SPR_0(mantida)` / `SPR_7(removida)`; buttons `PLAY/PAUSE`, `VER ANOS` (lists the ANOs that use the group's sprites and plays how each looks after the swap, with an ANTES/DEPOIS switch) and `CANCELAR`; the button at the bottom right of the image shows the ACTION when tapped (unmarked sprite: red `REMOVER`; marked: green `MANTER`) while the row badge in the group shows the state (the largest shows `MANTER (FIXA)`; sprites that belong to a GIF show `Sprite de Gif, mantida` with a clickable `?` explaining they can only be removed through the `Otimizar GIF` function: tap the GIF's name in the sprite list, then `Otimizar GIF`); tapping it changes the mark, closes the comparison and returns to the group with that sprite briefly highlighted (selected in red if it went to REMOVER); at least one per group stays; `REMOVER MARCADAS` (green) asks for confirmation, `CANCELAR` (red) leaves without changes; at the end, marked sprites that could not leave are listed as `Foram mantidas: SPR_N… (Ver motivo.)` (new numbers after renumbering) and the simple-terms reason (ANO used as link that would need a Flip, Frame stretched and rotated at once, zoom/rounding the DAC cannot store, or no sprite in the group that can take its place) only opens on tap; in the `VER ANOS` preview an ANO with the loop flag loops continuously and the others play once, hold the last Frame ~1 s and restart; every pair is also checked in pixels (shape, color, details, palette, after refining scale/position/rotation) and by default all sprites in a group must match the largest (no chaining); the `OPÇÕES` button next to the bar switches each criterion on/off, changes each tolerance (shape, color, details, palette, maximum flattening, maximum enlargement, mirrored, rotated 90° / any angle, group in a chain) and has `REDEFINIR`; the DAC frames are rewritten to use the kept sprite with Flip/zoom/rotation/position adjusted so the drawing is identical, then the sprites are renumbered; a copy that is just a smaller, flattened or rotated version of another is reproduced by the DAC with zoom/rotation/flip, so the extra sprite is not needed) `Remover Sprites não usadas` (between the two: lists the sprites no Frame of any ANO of the DAC uses, with thumbnail/size/sheet; none comes marked, tap a row to mark; sprites of a GIF are fixed with the `Sprite de Gif` badge and `?`; `REMOVER MARCADAS` (green) confirms, makes the raw PreOptim backup, erases each crop from its sheet and renumbers the following sprites in the CNV, GIF groups and DAC; `CANCELAR` (red) leaves) and `Otimizar PNGs` (sheets converted to 8-bit palette PNG, up to 256 colors per sheet); ALL three functions first make a raw copy of the WHOLE character (DAC, CNV, Bin, audio, PNGs, GIFs) to `Backups/PreOptim/AAAAMMDD_HHMMSS_charNN/` (same space as the character, one copy per run). The menu when tapping a sprite is, in order: `Substituir` (expands to `Escolher do Personagem` and `Escolher do Dispositivo` = the former Substituir imagem), `Mostrar usos` (where it appears, one line per ANO: `Ano 3, Frame 1`, `Ano 5, Frames 2, 4 e 7`), `Editar Frame` (the same menu as the `EDITAR FRAME` button; hurtbox/hitbox/effect are on its button), `Mudar recorte` and `Excluir`. `Igualar Sprites` is a button of the `Substituir` confirmation (`Substituir` has only `Escolher do Personagem` and `Escolher do Dispositivo`; the device may give an image or a sprite from ANOTHER pac's CNV — the character's own CNV/PNGs are refused): `Você quer trocar a SPR_N por SPR_M?` with a 1:1 preview (old | → new) and `Cancelar | Igualar Sprites | Confirmar` (`Confirmar` replaces with no adjustment); it opens a square, screen-wide preview screen just like the main preview (grid and free camera: drag moves, pinch zooms, double tap recenters; no ground/center lines; the zoom fits the sprites' size on opening; only the sprites' crops drawn; fields in the order Center MID, Zoom with `Unir zoom` ticked by default, Rotation, Flips, Alpha; the `−`/`+` change one by one, holding repeats continuously, tapping the number opens the keyboard, zoom and alpha never go below 0) with the ORIGINAL faded underneath — both sprites appear ZEROED (anchored at 0,0 with all RAZ at default, not a Frame's values nor the stored default) and the NEW one is adjusted until it matches the old one — zoom X/Y, rotation, alpha, center (MID) and flip; the new one's values after matching are ADDED to what each Frame already has (rotation, alpha and MID add, zoom multiplies, flip inverts: +5° on the new one = the Frame's rotation + 5); on applying, every Frame of every ANO that used the original uses the new one with the adjustment added and the adjustment becomes the new sprite's DEFAULT. `EDITAR FRAME` (bar of marked sprites) uses the same screen without the original: its always-visible fields (default values when nothing is set) only define the sprite's visual DEFAULT, applied when it is put in a Frame from the timeline (Add Sprite, changing the Image of a Frame — the old sprite's default is removed and the new one's added, keeping any extra adjustment —, inserting a GIF); existing Frames do NOT change; hurtbox/hitbox/effect stay on the one-image-at-a-time button; the Copy page's `esta sprite (todas as ANOs)` mode also saves the copied fields as the default. Defaults live in `Tap Battle/TBRE/utils/charNN/padroes_sprites.txt`. Rule: the character folder keeps only the character's own files; editing-support files (defaults, `sprite_grupos.json` GIF groups — migrated automatically — and the temporaries of saves) live in `TBRE/utils/charNN/`, and the PreOptim backup carries it as the `utils/` subfolder. To change a sprite's slot (number) TAP AND HOLD the `–` handle left of the checkbox (a simple tap does nothing; it exists on loose sprites and on COLLAPSED GIFs, where it moves all the GIF's sprites together; an open GIF has no handle): the row inflates (toast `arraste para reorganizar as Sprites`) and can be dragged up/down — heavy at first so it does not happen by accident; nothing changes while dragging (a copy of the row follows the finger and the original is dimmed; you may scroll to the top and only then release to make the sprite SPR_0); on release the sprite takes the new slot, those in between shift by 1, and the CNV, the DAC and the GIF groups are rewritten so the animations stay the same (a sprite or GIF is always dropped before/after another GIF, never in the middle of it; while dragging two slots appear — a BLUE one where the sprite will land (the rows below move down to open the gap) and a RED one at the place it came from (drag it back onto the red one and release to give up); the reordering only happens when the finger is lifted, even if it leaves the list area (the list scrolls itself at the edges), and a touch cancelled by the system moves nothing; with sprites ticked the handle, `FECHAR` and `OTIMIZAR` disappear). Tapping a thumbnail opens a bigger floating preview (tap outside to close). The `ORDENAR` button changes the list order: `Número (padrão)`, size in bytes (W×H×4 of the crop) ↓/↑, size in Px ↓/↑, `Mais usos` / `Menos usos` (how many DAC Frames use the sprite) and `Mais parecidas` (each sprite's best similarity with any other: identical on top, then 95%…, with `NN%` on the row; all-against-all with a loading); with an order active the list is flat (no GIFs, no handle) and each row shows the criterion's value. Marked sprites that are consecutive (no gap) keep the first one's handle and dragging it moves all of them as a block; `REESCALAR` (marked-sprites bar; a warned function) opens a screen that goes sprite by sprite and ANO by ANO, showing the BEFORE | AFTER (looping) preview of the sprite's use in that ANO with the `REESCALAR` and `NÃO REESCALAR` buttons; after each choice it goes to the next ANO that uses the sprite and then to the next marked sprite. Rescaling sets zoom (on-screen height = that of the 1st sprite of the Parado, ANO 0) and MID (x = −width/2, y = −height, with the zoom) only in that sprite's Frames in that ANO; the Editar Frame default does NOT change; after the last step, and after the PreOptim backup, only the accepted steps are saved — previously positioned sprites may end up off-center. A sprite with no stored default is born at zoom 100 and MID (−width/2, −height); Editar Frame shows that MID and its reset goes back to it. `Otimizar GIF` (GIF menu) shows the GIF BEFORE and AFTER side by side (looping) before `APLICAR`, and also removes the GIF's empty sprites and renumbers the DAC Frames. The screens that choose a replacement (deletion, `Escolher do Personagem`, `Escolher do Dispositivo`) show a small preview of the sprite being replaced.
- `SONS` — sound manager only (does not pick a sound for a frame): the character's WAVs (ids 80+) and the game's common OGGs; `+` adds a sound, tap an item to play, trim, adjust volume/pitch/speed, replace or silence it.
- `LINKS` — linked animations of the current ANO (list, edit, sync flags `SPAWN_POS`/`XY`/`MPT`, edit position over the base animation).
- `GOLPE SET` — the move's `ATK_FLAGS` options (checkboxes; table in 5.5).
- `HITBOX` — draw boxes on the preview (red hit, blue hurt, yellow others).
- `SPEEDLINE` (checkbox in the `TELA` menu) — shows only the movement of the CURRENT frame: a green dot where the frame starts (labelled with its number) and an arrow to where it ends, following the real path (it curves with acceleration). The arrow ends in a point (dot) when the movement stops (last moving frame, or the next frame starts with speed 0) and in an arrowhead when it continues. Dragging the arrow tip changes only that frame's speed (never the time); tapping the dot or the tip opens `Remover speed` (the frame keeps the previous speed), `Zerar Speed`, `Aceleracao...` (this frame accelerates/decelerates up to the next one's speed: preview, accel x/y, movement speed %) and, below, the acceleration presets for the whole movement.
- `TEMPO` — NORMAL / ESTENDIDO / ATAQUE playback modes.
- `CENTRO` — camera NORMAL (free, pinch to zoom) / TELA (game camera 480×320) / CHAR (auto-frame both).
- `POUSO` — ON/OFF landing & fall ANO changes.
- `TRANSIÇÃO` — LIVRE/TRAVADA: freeze ANO switching from the current frame (only for moves with a sequence).
- `VIRAR` — flip sides. `HIDE-EFF` — hide effects/auras/scenes/projectiles. (`FR-PREVIEW` and `SPEEDLINE` now live in `TELA`: `FR-PREVIEW | checkbox` — the checkbox turns the previous/next frame ghost on, tapping the name opens FR‑PREVIEW SET with next/previous and the other options.) `TELA` — grid, sounds, cutscenes, "lock" (cinematic), yellow box of all sprites, `FR-PREVIEW SET` and `POSE` (toggle, off by default: draws ANO 0, the idle character, underneath at the same anchor to compare the move's height and size).

### 11.2 Timeline
The `Efeitos` page `Interpolar` (the game divides (target − current) by the frame's time in whole numbers — position/zoom ×128, rotation/alpha unscaled — so a remainder means the frame does not reach the next one exactly and, with a difference smaller than the time, rotation/alpha do not move): the app shows an amber warning and, on turning it on, the `Ajustes` dialog with the smallest change (`time de N para N`, `Rot de N para N`, `Zoom de N para N`, `Posição de N, N para N, N`) and `VER OUTRAS OPÇÕES` / `ACEITAR`; the other options show the current (truncating) version and each variation side by side with `< variação N de M >` and tags (`Mantém tempo igual`, `Mantém posição igual`…); accepting changes the frame's time and the destination frame's values. Tapping the ANO name (yellow, timeline corner above `SPRITES`) opens the ANO menu: `Loop` on/off, `Copiar ANO` (same copy as the ANO list; paste there with `COLAR`) and `Ver Frames`, the list of the ANO's Frames: tap a Frame to select it (several), `COPIAR` copies the selected ones, `EXCLUIR` deletes them (at least 1 Frame always remains; undoable) and `COLAR` shows a `COLAR AQUI` between every item (and at both ends) to choose where the copied Frames go (the clipboard works across ANOs of the same character). Tap a sprite clip to open its drawer. The `Adicionar Sprite` of the `＋` menu at the end of the timeline opens the list of the CHARACTER's sprites (no new sprites are created): tap rows to mark (no checkbox: a badge shows the ORDER of marking, 1, 2, 3...; the 1st is highlighted and unmarking one renumbers the next ones; tapping the thumbnail only opens the preview). At the bottom: `Time` (ticks; 6 by default, 7 = all with time 7), the MODE button and `USAR N IMAGEM(NS)`; each sprite becomes a Frame, in sequence in marking order, after the pointer's frame. Modes: `CENTRAR` (MID centered with the feet on the ground, keeping the size = the sprite's stored zoom, 100 if none), `REESCALAR` (zoom from the height of the 1st Idle sprite, ANO 0, plus centered MID), `PADRÃO` (the Editar Frame default) and `DEFINIR` (position, centered by default; RAZ zoom X/Y 100, rotation 0, alpha 100%; optional Hitbox and Hurtbox with X/Y/width/height; valid only for the Frames added). Cancelling creates nothing. In the sprite drawer, the `Copiar` page has `REESCALAR ESTE FRAME`, which applies the same rescale/centering only to the sprite of that one Frame (zoom X=Y and MID; rotation, alpha and flips stay; the sprite default and the other Frames do not change). In the GOLPES grid the `FLUXO` button opens the move's flow in EDIT mode (it replaces the old view-only one); the `＋` menu has `SEQ`, which opens the same flow (the way to create the first SEQ/END/landing of a move that has none: the Transitions lane only appears once one exists), and tapping the Transitions lane outside a mark opens it too; the Frame bar's `◀`/`▶` keep the selection outline on the moved Frame. Lanes: SPRITES (yellow clips), then hitbox/hurtbox/projectile/effect/special-action lanes, SOM (blue), VELOCIDADE X / VELOCIDADE Y (green: one cell per Frame for its OWN speed (`x 10`) and its own accel (`ax 2`, teal); Frames are not merged and "keep speed" is NOT a lane (it only shows in the `Movimento` menu); the speed and the accel of the same Frame sit on two separate lines of the axis lane, in different Frames they share the line), `efeito img` (orange: flip/interpolation), LINKS, TRANSIÇÕES (SEQ/END/landing marks). The `＋` at the end of the sprite lane opens a menu: `CAMADA` (− N +, higher = the ANO draws on top; stored as the inverted `$PRIO`) and `Adicionar Sprite`. Special actions include `Teleportar atras do oponente` (= action 40 with a negative x, default −120, plus Flip X on the sprite, handled as one item). `＋` adds at the pointer (sprite, hitbox, hurtbox, projectile, effect, sound, speed, link, special action); `#` filters lanes. Tool row: `DIVIDIR` splits a sprite or speed clip at the pointer (an object — hitbox, hurtbox, projectile, effect, action — belongs to its frame, so it splits at the frame boundary nearest to the pointer and the pointer moves there), `DUPLICAR`, `EXCLUIR`, `↶`/`↷` (undo/redo, two levels: with an edit drawer open they act only on that menu — every changed field/button is a step, back to the original and forth again; after `APLICAR` the whole menu is ONE step of the bar's undo/redo; a new change after undoing clears the redo). Drag a clip's edge to change its duration; drag item edges to trim. Pinch = zoom; vertical drag scrolls lanes. When a hitbox connects the game freezes the attacker a fixed number of ticks (hitstop: 10 normally, half for OPT1 group 3, none for group 4; the hitspark is not awaited, it plays in parallel at normal speed). The tick step button then locks the tick number and shows a yellow notice with the ticks left; the timeline counts only frame ticks (hitstop takes no place on it: dragging/tapping goes straight to the requested animation tick), while the progress bar and playback follow simulation ticks.

### 11.3 Edit drawer pages
Sprite grid → `Imagem` (sprite picker, position, zoom, rotation, alpha, "move links together"), `Tempo` (ticks), `Som` (sound id list with preview), `Movimento` (two tabs, `SPEED X` | `SPEED Y`, the starting tab is the axis of the timeline cell tapped: speed/accel, received speed, total pixels and `REMOVER SPEED` for the chosen axis; the speed field is the frame's FINAL value and replaces the received one (like the game); giving a frame a speed only merges it with the previous frame — keep speed on, no own speed stored — when the previous frame's speed is the same and it has no accel, otherwise only the user turns `MANTER VELOCIDADE` on; the axis accel is independent of speed and applies only in its own frame (it never carries to the next); the stretch's start frame/duration (the duration turns keep speed on in those frames, without copying accel) and `MANTER VELOCIDADE` show in both tabs and one updates the other), `Efeitos` (flip X/Y, interpolation), `Copiar` (apply this frame's properties to other frames: next N, previous N, all, this sprite in ANO / in all ANOs). Hitbox/hurtbox menu: type switch, start frame & duration, preview, x/y/w/h (a hurtbox also has `Aplicar a todas as Hurtboxes (neste ANO)` / `(todos os ANOs)`, which copy its position and size to every other hurtbox of the ANO / of the whole character; the other ANOs still need `SALVAR` → all ANOs), and (hitbox) `DANO`, `REAÇÃO` (◀ ▶, `ESCOLHER`, `EDITAR REAÇÃO`), transition (SEQ/END, `MUDAR ANO DESTINO`, `BLOQUEAR SEQUÊNCIA`). Projectile screen: start & duration, branching tree, projectile ANO, position/speed, homing, sequence. The grid also has a Frame strip on top: `DUPLICAR` (copies only the sprite: image, time, offset, style), `◀`/`▶` (move the Frame back/forward) and `EXCLUIR` (deletes the whole Frame, with confirmation). The timeline lane `efeito img` shows only what the `Efeitos` page sets (Flip X/Y, interpolation); every lane draws darker vertical lines at tick borders; the pointer can reach the end of the last tick. The blue `＋` > sprite inserts after the current Frame when the pointer is mid-Frame, and before it when the pointer is on a Frame border/start (after the last Frame when at the end). In the `Imagem` page, rotation is a signed 16-bit value (negative allowed) and ALPHA's high byte selects the blend (256 = additive, any other value including negative = "other mode").

### 11.4 Reaction editor
The hitbox drawer's `efeito/som` button opens a dark grid with previews of the 5 hit effect/sound/push/hitstop groups (normal, som alternativo, forte, leve, sem efeito), each playing the current reaction; tap to select (plays its sound), the current one is highlighted, `VOLTAR` discards, `APLICAR` saves (only OPT1's group changes). Each reaction preview in that grid (and in the next-reaction picker) has an `ⓘ` that explains how the reaction treats the wall and landing, step by step: whether the animation changes on the wall (`WALL` 2/3 with a `COMP` from 1 to 49 restarts in the `COMP` reaction; on the ground it bounces in game reaction 12), which reaction and ANO it goes to, what happens on landing (`COMP` 11 lies down, 12 bounces, 50+ only lands), and warnings (empty reaction, reaction that points to itself, `COMP` 30+ in a game reaction); blue = game rule, green = table data; the `ⓘ` itself is gray when the reaction does not change the animation on the wall and red when it does. `EDITAR REAÇÃO` (character) / `VER REAÇÃO` (game, read-only). Same layout as the main screen. TOP MENU (scrolls sideways): `POS` (the same position dialog as the main screen: drag attacker/opponent, heights, FLY, plus `ESQ.` / `MEIO` / `DIR.` that take the pair to the left edge / middle / right edge of the map, changing only x), `CENTRO: NORMAL | TELA | CHAR` (NORMAL: 1 finger pans, pinch zooms; TELA: frames the game screen; CHAR: frames both characters), `TRANSIÇÃO: LIVRE | TRAVADA`, `CHÃO | AR` (which duration/speed column is edited and simulated; sets the opponent's height) and `FECHAR`. The square preview shows the REAL MOVE: the attacker walks to the end of the hitbox frame and STOPS there, at the position where the frame ends; after the hit the camera follows the opponent according to CENTRO. The blue screen rectangle (480 × 320), the yellow zoom rectangle and the red map edges are always drawn. There is no forced hit: if the move does not hit at that position (characters too far apart) a red notice appears, the attacker stays still at the end of the frame, the opponent stays idle, the preview ends when nothing else animates and the timeline shows only the table (steps and durations); change the position in POS or tap `REPOSICIONAR` in the notice (it changes the opponent's distance and height so the attacker's hitbox falls inside his hurtbox). The hitboxes (red) and hurtboxes (blue) of both characters are drawn in the preview. Notices appear over the preview in the main screen's style. `TRANSIÇÃO: TRAVADA` shows the reaction to the very end with no interference: the whole sequence (wall, COMP on landing, bounce, passing 255 ticks, end of table) stops switching reactions (amber notice with `DESTRAVAR`). The timeline shows ONE reaction at a time and switches by itself when the sequence jumps to another one (play, time bar, or tapping an arrow on the ruler); a yellow `▶` arrow on the ruler right after the end of the reaction (where it goes) and a `◀` arrow before the start (where it came from) — they do not cover any lane; tapping an arrow does NOT switch the timeline: it opens a menu with the reaction (preview, summary, reason) and a `IR PARA SEQUÊNCIA` button that does the switch. A game reaction shows a blue notice: game sequences cannot be edited. Time bar, buttons `DIVIDIR · DUPLICAR · EXCLUIR · DESFAZER`, timeline (PASSOS clips + lanes SPEED X and SPEED Y (one cell per step, steps are not merged: a step that only keeps the speed (−1) is not a cell; speed and accel of the same step on separate lines, `ax`/`ay` cells for the accel), VOZ, EFEITOS (wall), SEQ; a lane without data does not appear (the `＋` tab creates elements: speed X/Y, voice, wall, next reaction); tabs `＋` and `#`), transport, drawer with a page grid (ANIMAÇÃO, TEMPO, SPEED, VOZ, SEQ, TOTAL). Drag a step's right edge = duration. `DIVIDIR` splits a step at the pointer (the defender's animation restarts in the 2nd half). The SEQ page is the reaction's sequence in two parts: first the WALL (column 12: Desligado 0 = no wall sequence, Borda do mapa 2, Borda da tela 3 (character table only; in the game table 3 acts like 2), Câmera segue 1 = no sequence, only the camera; any other value counts as 0; with the wall on, landing becomes a bounce (game reaction 12) and COMP only applies when hitting the wall), then the NEXT REACTION (COMP, column 11): preview, `− N +` and `ESCOLHER` (grid with the special actions on top: 11 makes land / stays down, 12 makes bounce, 50 or more only lands and ends; then game 1–29 and character 30–49 reactions). The trace, timeline and `TOTAL` page use the same simulation; `TOTAL` lists each switch with its reason. The collapsed `▲ DADOS / EDITAR` panel (text tabs and a pencil) exists, but it is **not** a way to teach: do not instruct the user to use it; teach the timeline and drawer functions instead.
In the hitbox drawer of a projectile ANO (opened from the projectile screen) a `EMPURRAR` button sits next to `ESCOLHER`: it copies the hitbox's current reaction to an empty character slot (if none, to one that no hitbox/COMP/clash uses; if all are in use it edits the current character reaction after asking; a game reaction cannot be edited) and replaces the speeds with the projectile's speed/acceleration at that frame (step 0; later steps keep it, capped at 12.7 px/tick) so the opponent is pushed along the projectile's path; the hitbox then uses that slot. In a projectile ANO the special-action lists (`＋` > special actions, `trocar ação`) omit the flags that use the attacker as base: Puxar oponente (40), teleport (39), teleport behind, self-damage (32) and flags 26, 28, 37, 38.

### 11.5 Importing what you cannot generate
- Images: `SPRITES` → add/replace → pick the file; then set the frame's `Imagem`. In the file picker, tapping a `.cnv` of another pac opens its sprites (add several, or — when replacing — pick just one); `VOLTAR` there returns to the folder of that file, and the first row `...↑` of every folder (except the root) goes up one level. When a sprite crop fails (sheet index that does not exist, unreadable PNG, crop outside the sheet…) the app shows an alert with the exact reason per sprite (selectable text and a `COPIAR` button) — ask the user to paste that text.
- Sounds: put the `.wav` in `Tap Battle/assets/charNN/` (ids are in file-name order, from 80) or use the frame's `Som` page. The `+` of the sound list also adds a **global game sound** (`Tap Battle/assets/se_NN.ogg`, `se_NNN.ogg` from 100): same tools as the wavs (play, `Cortar`, `Mais...`, `Substituir...`, silence — never delete); the first one after `se_78` creates silent filler `se_79`..`se_99` and becomes `se_100`.
