## 11. Screens and buttons (what each control does)

### 11.0 Title bar
`↻` reloads the folders the app reads (asks for confirmation; unsaved edits are lost; keeps the same character, opponent and move when the folders still exist) — use it after the user adds, deletes or replaces a character folder or a `.txt` file. `PT` / `EN` / `ES` switches the app language instantly. `AJUDA` opens the in-app help (chapters, search, and the button that creates this file).

### 11.1 Top menu (16 buttons; the user can long-press and drag them to reorder; the first is pinned)
- `SALVAR` — save (section 13).
- `CHAR` — choose the **attacker** and the **opponent** characters; `POS` sets distance/height between them by dragging on screen (plus `ESQ.` / `MEIO` / `DIR.` to take the pair to the left edge / middle / right edge of the map, changing only x) (and `FLY`: attacker airborne); `CHAR OBJ` shows body box and sprite anchors and edits the body size. The +1 tick counter locks during hitstop and the cinematic lock and then continues at locked+1 (frozen ticks do not count). The ANO/move selector is a button that opens a grid. A `GOLPES | ANOS` switch at the top changes mode on the spot. The grid shows the first Frame of each item; tapping a card expands it and plays the animation without opening (landing simulation for aerial moves; loop for ANOs with wActFlag 1, others play once, hold the last Frame 1 s and restart; the hit/projectile box is drawn); the big `➜ ABRIR` button opens it for editing. In ANO mode: `FILTRAR` cycles Padrão / Importância / Golpes / Ataque / Tempo / Frames / Efeitos (reorders without hiding: matches go first with a green frame); at the bottom `＋ CRIAR` (new ANO = last+1, one frame IMG -1 with 6 ticks), `EXCLUIR` (empties the ANO, it keeps existing; warns if it has a hitbox/projectile or belongs to a BIN attack), `COPIAR`, `MOVER` (pick another ANO; the Frames go there and the original becomes empty) and `COLAR` (same warning); holding a card starts selection mode to delete several. The `ⓘ` button: on an ANO only its role (`ANIM_INICIAL do Golpe N`, `SEQ do golpe N`, `Projétil do golpe N`, `Animação de pouso padrão`, fixed game ANOs such as `Parado` or `Carga de ki`; ANOs 29–39 are the normal A attacks and 30/32 have the OPT 512 combo window); on a move, the button that triggers it with its icon from effect.pac plus a `Sequências:` block. Colors: blue = fixed in the game's source code, green = from the BIN or another source. Move block: green = BIN (`SEQ (chão/ar)`, `END (chão/ar)`, `SEQ PROJ` = `ANIM_AO_ACERTAR`, grab `OPONENTE_AGARRADO`); blue with `(automática)` = code (special repeat ANO 42/40-41 for moves 0/3/6 with `MAX_REPETICOES`, grab → `ANIM_INICIAL + 1`); landing is not listed. ANOs 29–39 show their continuation: ANO 30 → A:32, A+forward:31, A+back:37, A+down:36; ANO 32 → A:33 and the same directions; the others `Sem continuação`. Game buttons (`CreateGamepad` + `Controller`; pad key = 6th `AddPad` argument): A (effect.pac ANO 209) = normal attacks, fixed ANOs 29–39, not in HDAT 4 (ground: A neutral 30, forward 31, back 37, down 36, from a run 29; jumping: 34/35/39/38; combo window OPT 512 in ANOs 30/32: 30→32→33, or 31/36/37 with a direction); Y (330) = moves 0/3/6; X (333) = 1/4/7; B (336) = 2/5/8 (ground / jumping-falling / flying); Z (339, 100 Synchro = 1 ki bar) = moves 9 (ground/flying) and 10 (jump); IRA (ANO 45, no button of its own: tap of the corner button while `AngryOn`, 200 Synchro) = moves 11 (ground/flying) and 12 (air); the same tap without `AngryOn` and Angry >= 100 = Rage = moves 14/15. Y, X, B and Z exist only in the game's button layout (`iControlType == 1`); otherwise the specials come from a joystick gesture **followed by the A button** (the game only reads the gesture on the tick A is pressed, `JoyTrig & 670056704`): Y `↓ → + A`, X `→ ↓ + A`, B `↓ ← + A`, Z `↓ ← → + A`, IRA `↓ ← ↓ ← + A` (with `AngryOn`). Special moves 9-12 have the game's cinematic lock: the +1 tick button shows a yellow notice with a `PULAR` (skip) button that passes through the lock and releases the tick, and the camera zooms to 200% (4 ticks, centered on the owner: 40 px outward, 48 px above the feet) and back to 100% in 12 ticks after it. The attacker/opponent pickers are dark grids (3 columns) where each `charNN` plays its own ANO 76 (ANO 0 if it has none); the current one is highlighted. The opponent row is `OPONENTE: charNN | DEF`: the `DEF` checkbox (off by default) makes the opponent block, to test how the move behaves when blocked: no damage or reaction, guard stance (ANO 4, or 81 in the air), pushback 13 decaying 2 per tick, the OPT 1 hitstop, block effect ANO 2 and SE 8 (groups 0-3), 2 chip damage only when the hitting ANO is >= 40 and damage is not 0; hitbox flag 26 (enemy does not block) and grabs are not blocked, and a blocked hit still confirms the move (ANIM_SEQ/ANIM_END happen). With the opponent's `DEF` box on, a guarded hit does **not** confirm the hit (no SEQ / `ANIM_AO_ACERTAR` / landing-on-hit; those only happen when the move connects). Effects placed on the opponent's own frames (hit flags 71–125 → his character's ANO 201+, 10–17 effect, 41–70 charf; e.g. the visual of the defense on ANO 4) are created and drawn too.
- `SPRITES` — the character's sprite list: add, replace (the old sprite's pixels are erased from the sheet BEFORE the new one is placed, except what another sprite still uses, so a smaller sprite can take the same spot and the rest stays free and clean), remove (removing only takes it out of the CNV and RENUMBERS the following sprites: SPR_0 out → 1 becomes 0, and the CNV, GIF groups and every DAC frame follow), reorder, change the cut, import PNG/JPG/GIF; also choose the sprite for a frame. Its `OTIMIZAR` button (next to `+ ADICIONAR`) has two functions: `Remover Sprites repetidas` (groups equal/similar sprites, also mirrored, rotated 90° or at another scale; a minimum-similarity bar; you tick which to remove, at least one per group stays; the DAC frames are rewritten to use the kept sprite with Flip/zoom/rotation/position adjusted so the drawing is identical, then the sprites are renumbered) and `Otimizar PNGs` (sheets converted to 8-bit palette PNG, up to 256 colors per sheet, originals copied first to `Backups/PreOptim/charNN/`). Tapping a sprite offers `Mostrar usos` (right below `Substituir imagem`): where it appears, one line per ANO (`Ano 3, Frame 1`, `Ano 5, Frames 2, 4 e 7`).
- `SONS` — sound manager only (does not pick a sound for a frame): the character's WAVs (ids 80+) and the game's common OGGs; `+` adds a sound, tap an item to play, trim, adjust volume/pitch/speed, replace or silence it.
- `LINKS` — linked animations of the current ANO (list, edit, sync flags `SPAWN_POS`/`XY`/`MPT`, edit position over the base animation).
- `GOLPE SET` — the move's `ATK_FLAGS` options (checkboxes; table in 5.5).
- `HITBOX` — draw boxes on the preview (red hit, blue hurt, yellow others).
- `SPEEDLINE` (checkbox in the `TELA` menu) — shows only the movement of the CURRENT frame: a green dot where the frame starts (labelled with its number) and an arrow to where it ends, following the real path (it curves with acceleration). The arrow ends in a point (dot) when the movement stops (last moving frame, or the next frame starts with speed 0) and in an arrowhead when it continues. Dragging the arrow tip changes only that frame's speed (never the time); tapping the dot or the tip opens `Remover speed` (the frame keeps the previous speed), `Zerar Speed`, `Aceleracao...` (this frame accelerates/decelerates up to the next one's speed: preview, accel x/y, movement speed %) and, below, the acceleration presets for the whole movement.
- `TEMPO` — NORMAL / ESTENDIDO / ATAQUE playback modes.
- `CENTRO` — camera NORMAL (free, pinch to zoom) / TELA (game camera 480×320) / CHAR (auto-frame both).
- `POUSO` — ON/OFF landing & fall ANO changes.
- `TRANSIÇÃO` — LIVRE/TRAVADA: freeze ANO switching from the current frame (only for moves with a sequence).
- `VIRAR` — flip sides. `HIDE-EFF` — hide effects/auras/scenes/projectiles. (`FR-PREVIEW` and `SPEEDLINE` now live in `TELA`: `FR-PREVIEW | checkbox` — the checkbox turns the previous/next frame ghost on, tapping the name opens FR‑PREVIEW SET with next/previous and the other options.) `TELA` — grid, sounds, cutscenes, "lock" (cinematic), yellow box of all sprites, `FR-PREVIEW SET` and `POSE` (toggle, off by default: draws ANO 0, the idle character, underneath at the same anchor to compare the move's height and size).

### 11.2 Timeline
Tap a sprite clip to open its drawer. Lanes: SPRITES (yellow clips), then hitbox/hurtbox/projectile/effect/special-action lanes, SOM (blue), VELOCIDADE X / VELOCIDADE Y (green: one cell per Frame, Frames that keep the speed are NOT merged — the inherited value shows as `x =10` —, and the speed and the accel (`ax`, teal) of the same Frame sit on two separate lines of the axis lane; in different Frames they share the line), `efeito img` (orange: flip/keep speed/interpolation), LINKS, TRANSIÇÕES (SEQ/END/landing marks). The `＋` at the end of the sprite lane opens a menu: `CAMADA` (− N +, higher = the ANO draws on top; stored as the inverted `$PRIO`) and `Adicionar Sprite`. Special actions include `Teleportar atras do oponente` (= action 40 with a negative x, default −120, plus Flip X on the sprite, handled as one item). `＋` adds at the pointer (sprite, hitbox, hurtbox, projectile, effect, sound, speed, link, special action); `#` filters lanes. Tool row: `DIVIDIR` splits a sprite or speed clip at the pointer (an object — hitbox, hurtbox, projectile, effect, action — belongs to its frame, so it splits at the frame boundary nearest to the pointer and the pointer moves there), `DUPLICAR`, `EXCLUIR`, `↶`/`↷` (undo/redo, two levels: with an edit drawer open they act only on that menu — every changed field/button is a step, back to the original and forth again; after `APLICAR` the whole menu is ONE step of the bar's undo/redo; a new change after undoing clears the redo). Drag a clip's edge to change its duration; drag item edges to trim. Pinch = zoom; vertical drag scrolls lanes. When a hitbox connects the game freezes the attacker a fixed number of ticks (hitstop: 10 normally, half for OPT1 group 3, none for group 4; the hitspark is not awaited, it plays in parallel at normal speed). The tick step button then locks the tick number and shows a yellow notice with the ticks left; the timeline counts only frame ticks (hitstop takes no place on it: dragging/tapping goes straight to the requested animation tick), while the progress bar and playback follow simulation ticks.

### 11.3 Edit drawer pages
Sprite grid → `Imagem` (sprite picker, position, zoom, rotation, alpha, "move links together"), `Tempo` (ticks), `Som` (sound id list with preview), `Movimento` (two tabs, `SPEED X` | `SPEED Y`, the starting tab is the axis of the timeline cell tapped: speed/accel, received speed, total pixels and `REMOVER SPEED` for the chosen axis; the stretch's start frame/duration and `MANTER VELOCIDADE` show in both tabs and one updates the other), `Efeitos` (flip X/Y, interpolation), `Copiar` (apply this frame's properties to other frames: next N, previous N, all, this sprite in ANO / in all ANOs). Hitbox/hurtbox menu: type switch, start frame & duration, preview, x/y/w/h (a hurtbox also has `Aplicar a todas as Hurtboxes (neste ANO)` / `(todos os ANOs)`, which copy its position and size to every other hurtbox of the ANO / of the whole character; the other ANOs still need `SALVAR` → all ANOs), and (hitbox) `DANO`, `REAÇÃO` (◀ ▶, `ESCOLHER`, `EDITAR REAÇÃO`), transition (SEQ/END, `MUDAR ANO DESTINO`, `BLOQUEAR SEQUÊNCIA`). Projectile screen: start & duration, branching tree, projectile ANO, position/speed, homing, sequence. The grid also has a Frame strip on top: `DUPLICAR` (copies only the sprite: image, time, offset, style), `◀`/`▶` (move the Frame back/forward) and `EXCLUIR` (deletes the whole Frame, with confirmation). The timeline lane `efeito img` shows only what the `Efeitos` page sets (Flip X/Y, keep speed, interpolation); every lane draws darker vertical lines at tick borders; the pointer can reach the end of the last tick. The blue `＋` > sprite inserts after the current Frame when the pointer is mid-Frame, and before it when the pointer is on a Frame border/start (after the last Frame when at the end). In the `Imagem` page, rotation is a signed 16-bit value (negative allowed) and ALPHA's high byte selects the blend (256 = additive, any other value including negative = "other mode").

### 11.4 Reaction editor
The hitbox drawer's `efeito/som` button opens a dark grid with previews of the 5 hit effect/sound/push/hitstop groups (normal, som alternativo, forte, leve, sem efeito), each playing the current reaction; tap to select (plays its sound), the current one is highlighted, `VOLTAR` discards, `APLICAR` saves (only OPT1's group changes). `EDITAR REAÇÃO` (character) / `VER REAÇÃO` (game, read-only). Same layout as the main screen. TOP MENU (scrolls sideways): `POS` (the same position dialog as the main screen: drag attacker/opponent, heights, FLY, plus `ESQ.` / `MEIO` / `DIR.` that take the pair to the left edge / middle / right edge of the map, changing only x), `CENTRO: NORMAL | TELA | CHAR` (NORMAL: 1 finger pans, pinch zooms; TELA: frames the game screen; CHAR: frames both characters), `TRANSIÇÃO: LIVRE | TRAVADA`, `CHÃO | AR` (which duration/speed column is edited and simulated; sets the opponent's height) and `FECHAR`. The square preview shows the REAL MOVE: the attacker walks to the end of the hitbox frame and STOPS there, at the position where the frame ends; after the hit the camera follows the opponent according to CENTRO. The blue screen rectangle (480 × 320), the yellow zoom rectangle and the red map edges are always drawn. There is no forced hit: if the move does not hit at that position (characters too far apart) a red notice appears, the attacker stays still at the end of the frame, the opponent stays idle, the preview ends when nothing else animates and the timeline shows only the table (steps and durations); change the position in POS or tap `REPOSICIONAR` in the notice (it changes the opponent's distance and height so the attacker's hitbox falls inside his hurtbox). The hitboxes (red) and hurtboxes (blue) of both characters are drawn in the preview. Notices appear over the preview in the main screen's style. `TRANSIÇÃO: TRAVADA` shows the reaction to the very end with no interference: the whole sequence (wall, COMP on landing, bounce, passing 255 ticks, end of table) stops switching reactions (amber notice with `DESTRAVAR`). The timeline shows ONE reaction at a time and switches by itself when the sequence jumps to another one (play, time bar, or tapping an arrow on the ruler); a yellow `▶` arrow on the ruler right after the end of the reaction (where it goes) and a `◀` arrow before the start (where it came from) — they do not cover any lane; tapping an arrow does NOT switch the timeline: it opens a menu with the reaction (preview, summary, reason) and a `IR PARA SEQUÊNCIA` button that does the switch. A game reaction shows a blue notice: game sequences cannot be edited. Time bar, buttons `DIVIDIR · DUPLICAR · EXCLUIR · DESFAZER`, timeline (PASSOS clips + lanes SPEED X and SPEED Y (one cell per step, steps are not merged: a step that keeps the speed (−1) shows `x =10` with the effective value; speed and accel of the same step on separate lines, `ax`/`ay` cells for the accel), VOZ, EFEITOS (wall), SEQ; a lane without data does not appear (the `＋` tab creates elements: speed X/Y, voice, wall, next reaction); tabs `＋` and `#`), transport, drawer with a page grid (ANIMAÇÃO, TEMPO, SPEED, VOZ, SEQ, TOTAL). Drag a step's right edge = duration. `DIVIDIR` splits a step at the pointer (the defender's animation restarts in the 2nd half). The SEQ page is the reaction's sequence in two parts: first the WALL (column 12: Desligado 0 = no wall sequence, Borda do mapa 2, Borda da tela 3 (character table only; in the game table 3 acts like 2), Câmera segue 1 = no sequence, only the camera; any other value counts as 0; with the wall on, landing becomes a bounce (game reaction 12) and COMP only applies when hitting the wall), then the NEXT REACTION (COMP, column 11): preview, `− N +` and `ESCOLHER` (grid with the special actions on top: 11 makes land / stays down, 12 makes bounce, 50 or more only lands and ends; then game 1–29 and character 30–49 reactions). The trace, timeline and `TOTAL` page use the same simulation; `TOTAL` lists each switch with its reason. The collapsed `▲ DADOS / EDITAR` panel (text tabs and a pencil) exists, but it is **not** a way to teach: do not instruct the user to use it; teach the timeline and drawer functions instead.
In the hitbox drawer of a projectile ANO (opened from the projectile screen) a `EMPURRAR` button sits next to `ESCOLHER`: it copies the hitbox's current reaction to an empty character slot (if none, to one that no hitbox/COMP/clash uses; if all are in use it edits the current character reaction after asking; a game reaction cannot be edited) and replaces the speeds with the projectile's speed/acceleration at that frame (step 0; later steps keep it, capped at 12.7 px/tick) so the opponent is pushed along the projectile's path; the hitbox then uses that slot. In a projectile ANO the special-action lists (`＋` > special actions, `trocar ação`) omit the flags that use the attacker as base: Puxar oponente (40), teleport (39), teleport behind, self-damage (32) and flags 26, 28, 37, 38.

### 11.5 Importing what you cannot generate
- Images: `SPRITES` → add/replace → pick the file; then set the frame's `Imagem`. In the file picker, tapping a `.cnv` of another pac opens its sprites (add several, or — when replacing — pick just one); `VOLTAR` there returns to the folder of that file, and the first row `...↑` of every folder (except the root) goes up one level. When a sprite crop fails (sheet index that does not exist, unreadable PNG, crop outside the sheet…) the app shows an alert with the exact reason per sprite (selectable text and a `COPIAR` button) — ask the user to paste that text.
- Sounds: put the `.wav` in `Tap Battle/assets/charNN/` (ids are in file-name order, from 80) or use the frame's `Som` page. The `+` of the sound list also adds a **global game sound** (`Tap Battle/assets/se_NN.ogg`, `se_NNN.ogg` from 100): same tools as the wavs (play, `Cortar`, `Mais...`, `Substituir...`, silence — never delete); the first one after `se_78` creates silent filler `se_79`..`se_99` and becomes `se_100`.
