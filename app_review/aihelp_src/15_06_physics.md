### 15.6 Physics and movement (questions 46–52)

**Coordinates [CODE].** Fixed point 16.16 for `PlayerXPos`, `PlayerYPos`, `obj.x/y` and the animation `iXSpeed/iYSpeed/iXAccel/iYAccel` (32-bit little-endian in the pattern, jogo:31913-31937). Ground is `PlayerYPos = 15728640` (240 px); **Y grows downward**; `Y > ground` is below the floor. Visible screen width 31391744 (479 px), world width 62849024 (959 px), `iBaseXPos` starts at 31457280 (jogo:5554), scroll zones at 4587520 and 26869760. `obj.x = PlayerXPos - iBackXPos (- iBackXOffset)`. Half width `hw = charHDAT3[0][3]/2` px. There is **no global gravity and no speed clamp** in the engine; gravity is a per-pattern `iYAccel` in the animation script or the accel columns of a reaction row.

**Order of update inside one tick [CODE].**
- *Animation script* (`_ActReqMain`, jogo:31804-31842): `x += iXSpeed` (or `-=` when `wObjFlag & 16`, facing left), `y += iYSpeed` (or `-=` when `wObjFlag & 32`), then `iXSpeed += iXAccel`, `iYSpeed += iYAccel`, then `--time`. A pattern of duration T gets T moves with speeds s0, s0+a, s0+2a…; the load tick moves nothing. At pattern load speed is zeroed unless `wActFlag & 64` (keep speed) and accel is **always** zeroed (jogo:31901-31907).
- *Reaction (md 876/877)*: on the entry tick speeds are loaded and the position moves with them; on later ticks (hitstop = 0) it **accelerates first** (`speed += accel`) **then moves** — the opposite of animation scripts. **X friction**: after accelerating X, if the sign flipped, or the old value was ≤ 0 and the new is > 0, or the new is 0 → `DamageXSpeed = 0` and `DamageXAccel = 0` (jogo:21075-21078), so X cannot accelerate from rest. Y has no clamp. `+speed` moves in the facing direction (flag 16 → `X -= speed<<9`, jogo:21082-21088); defenders face the attacker, so knock-back uses negative speeds unless the reaction ANO is negative (flipped).
- *Per-tick sequence for a defender*: reaction speed update → reaction move → `PlayerTail` (jogo:29714-29899): applies `DamageMove`, `_SetXY`, `_ActReq` (animation script movement) and writes `PlayerXPos` back. So the **reaction speed and the animation-script speed both apply in the same tick**; then `CheckOptFlag` runs. `DamageMove` (guard push) moves `X ± DamageMove<<15` then `DamageMove -= 2` (floor 0).

**Ground, air, landing [CODE].** Airborne = `PlayerYPos != 15728640` and `PlayerAir`. Attack landing: md 883 checks `Y > ground && iYSpeed > 0` → SE 12, `Y = ground`, then the HDAT 4 landing ANO (`POUSO_AO_ERRAR` / `POUSO_AO_ACERTAR`) or ANO 13 and md 893 (jogo:21958-21995). Free air movement in md 886 snaps to the ground and enters md 887 (jogo:22141-22147). Falling after an airborne animation ends: ANO 89 (fixed).

**Screen and walls [CODE].** Camera task md 19: clamp `X ∈ [iBackXPos+hw, iBackXPos+31391744-hw]` when `iBackXOffset == 0`, no scroll target, not md 911..915 and not `ScreenEdgeOff` (jogo:15537-15562; `ScreenEdgeOff` is set by cutscene kinds 7/8/9). World-edge test uses `iBaseXPos == 0` or `62849024`. Grounded players are kept apart by `(w0+w1)/2` (`ATK_FLAGS` bit 16 "GHOST" disables that separation near the floor). Corner push-back: a player pushed into the edge while the opponent is attacking pushes the opponent back by 80 % of the penetration (jogo:15159-15171).

**Bounce, ricochet, continuous fall [CODE].** There is **no automatic velocity reflection**. A bounce is a new reaction row with its own speeds: gamedata type 12 on a WALL 2/3 landing, the `comp` types, type 13 at the end of a table, type 2 / duration 255 timeouts.

**Facing [CODE].** `wObjFlag & 16` = facing left (set for player 1 at start; bit 32 flips Y). It sets the movement sign of animation scripts, the hitbox mirror (`(wActFlag & 48) ^ (wObjFlag & 48)`, jogo:2058-2096) and projectile/effect spawn sides. `DamageChange` turns the defender toward the attacker (unless it is within half-width of the matching screen edge and facing the wall); `StartPlayerAttack` turns the attacker toward the opponent (jogo:31625-31634). A negative reaction ANO flips again.

**Sources of movement [CODE].** (1) the attacker's animation script (speed/accel/keep-bit); (2) the defender's reaction rows; (3) projectile scripts (md 971: `_work[2]/[3]` world-absolute; `obj.x = _work[2] - iBackXPos - iBackXOffset`, `_ActReq`, back to `_work`; facing from the owner; optional homing; clamped to `Y <= ground` then a ground-impact animation or death); (4) teleports: hit flags 39/40 (jogo:2314-2337) and cutscene kinds 10..15 (jogo:25244-25268); (5) guard push `DamageMove`; (6) `PlayerTail` separation/clamps.

**Minimum parameters to reproduce a move's physics without simulating the scene [CODE].** Attack/projectile: per pattern the duration (`$TIME`), `$MOVE` speed/accel (px per tick as the editor prints them) and the keep-speed bit, the facing, start position and the floor. Reaction: per row `DUR_C/DUR_A`, ground and air `xs, ys, xa, ya`, `COMP`, `WALL`, `ANO`, plus the chain through `COMP`, hitstop, facing, start `X/Y`. Not data-driven: screen/wall geometry (half width from HDAT 3, the constants above) and the guard/KO decisions.
