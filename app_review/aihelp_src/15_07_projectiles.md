### 15.7 Projectiles and sub-projectiles (questions 53–63)

**Creation [CODE].** A projectile appears when the attacker's current pattern contains a hit-box entry of **type 0** and `BulletAction[p][0] != 0 && BulletNum < 8` (`PlayerTail`, jogo:29808-29878). `BulletAction[p][0]` was chosen at `StartPlayerAttack` (jogo:31664-31672) from HDAT 4: byte 4 (`PROJETIL_ANIM_NORMAL`) when `PlayerYPos[att] < PlayerYPos[opp] + 3342336` and `> PlayerYPos[opp] - 2424832`, byte 34 (`PROJETIL_ANIM_ABAIXO`) when the attacker is ≥ 51 px lower, byte 36 (`PROJETIL_ANIM_ACIMA`) when ≥ 37 px higher. `BulletAction[p][1]` = byte 6 (`PROJETIL_ANIM_AO_ACERTAR`). The spawned task md 970 (prio 13312+p) does `_SetAct(p+3, ano)` and `_SetObj(153+4p)` (jogo:24534-24535): **the projectile's animation is an ordinary ANO of the same character DAC** (`ChrGameData[p+3]`). **Count**: 1 per type-0 entry; **3 projectiles** (ANO, ANO+1, ANO+2) when `SpecialKind & 4`. **Position**: attacker `PlayerXPos ± (hit.x << 16)` (mirrored by facing), `Y + (hit.y << 16)`; `SpecialKind & 32` → at the opponent's X/Y; `& 64` → opponent's X on the ground. After spawning, the hit entry's type is set to −1 (spawns once per pattern). Limits: `BulletNum < 8` per player, and at most 16 registered hit-capable objects per owner (`iTCBReqNum < 16`).

**Loop md 971 [CODE]** (jogo:24545-24698). World-absolute position in `_work[2]/[3]`; each tick `obj.x = _work[2] - iBackXPos - iBackXOffset`, `_ActReq` (the ANO script moves it with `$MOVE` speed/accel — **the projectile's speed, direction and trajectory are the ANO's `$XSPEED/$YSPEED/$XACCEL/$YACCEL`**, mirrored by the owner's facing stored in `_work[6]`), then back to `_work`. Death when its ANO ends (`wActFlag & 2`, jogo:24549) or on contact rules below. `Y` is clamped to the ground: touching it switches to the floor ANO (`_work[13]` = byte 40 `PROJETIL_COLISAO_CHAO`) or dies (jogo:24575-24598). It runs `CheckOptFlag` unless frozen, so a projectile ANO can carry hit-flag actions (shake, dust, sub-projectile spawn…), but those land on the **owner's** `PlayerState`.

**Hit handling [CODE].** Collision is a normal attack-box vs hurtbox test (`tcbReqHit[]`, jogo:23383-23428); skipped if either player's `wObjFlag & 8` or a counter/clash already happened this tick. On a hit: `DamageChange` with the **owner's** `AttackMul`/flags and the projectile's current `OptData[0]` (damage) and `OptData[1]` (reaction); target hit-stop = full, projectile hit-stop = half, owner not frozen; `HitTarget[tgt] = 1`; `DamageHitSpecialType[tgt] = _work[11]`. If the projectile's SpecialType equals the owner's current one: `CancelTime = 20` and `wFlag |= 4096` on the owner (jogo:24560-24566), which makes the **attacker** switch to the alternate hit ANO (`ANIM_AO_ACERTAR`, byte 32 — "SEQ_PROJETIL") and then arm SEQ/END. After a hit the projectile continues if `SpecialKind & 1` (the editor's "does not explode on the enemy"/GROUND_ONLY, jogo:24569), otherwise it switches to the hit ANO (`_work[12]`, byte 38 `PROJETIL_ANIM_ACERTAR_GLOBAL`, or byte 6) or dies. Because each *pattern* with a hit block hits once, **a projectile ANO with several hit-block patterns hits several times** (multi-hit "beams"); a pattern without a hit block cannot hit.

**Sub-projectiles [CODE].** A type-0 entry inside a projectile ANO spawns a sub-projectile with ANO from byte 18 (`SUBPROJETIL_ANIM_ID`), on-hit ANO byte 20, floor ANO byte 42 (jogo:24658-24695).

**Homing and repeats [CODE].** Homing runs only when the bullet is not frozen **and HDAT4 byte 26 (`HOMING`) equals 0** (the field looks inverted: 0 = homing on); speed is byte 28; it uses an atan table and sin table (jogo:24610-24628) — it recomputes the heading every tick **[INFERRED]**. Repeats (md 883, jogo:21997-22028): while `SpecialBulletNumber < HDAT4[30]` (`MAX_REPETICOES`), the frame has `wOptFlag & 512` and the player presses the attack buttons (`JoyTrig & 16640`), ANO 40/41 are swapped and the counter increments; the repeat uses the 512 window, which the grab also uses (a move cannot do both).

**Interpretation of the user's GOLPE_9 (Kamehameha) question (Q63/Q90/Q91) [INFERRED from the generic rules].** The role of an ANO is decided **only by which `$GOLPE_n` field points to it**, never by its number. Read `$GOLPE_9`: `ANIM_INICIAL` (the attacker's cast ANO, here 51); `PROJETIL_ANIM_NORMAL/ABAIXO/ACIMA` (the projectile ANO — probably 49 — chosen by height); `PROJETIL_ANIM_AO_ACERTAR` / `ACERTAR_GLOBAL` / `COLISAO_CHAO` (ANOs the projectile switches to on hit/floor — possibly 50); `ANIM_AO_ACERTAR` (the attacker's reaction ANO after the beam connects). Inside the projectile ANO, each frame/pattern with a type-2 box and `$OPT` damage hits **once** per pattern: "damage 5 and 4, reaction 116, then a frame without hitbox" means the beam hits in the patterns that carry a hitbox, dealing the formula in 15.4 each time (5 and 4 are `d0` values, 116 = OPT1 group 2 / game reaction 16), with combo scaling reducing later hits, and the pattern without a hitbox cannot hit. The visual (sprites, `$IMG`, `$MID`, `$MOVE` speed) and the collision (type-2 boxes, `$OPT`) are separate data in the same frames.
**To change only…** *damage*: `$OPT` `d0` of the hitbox frames. *reaction*: `$OPT` `d1` (OPT1). *projectile speed*: `$XSPEED` (and accel) in the projectile ANO's `$MOVE`. *number of hits*: the number of patterns that carry a type-2 box (add/remove hit blocks), and `MAX_REPETICOES` for repeated throws. *impact animation*: `PROJETIL_ANIM_AO_ACERTAR` / `ACERTAR_GLOBAL` (what the projectile becomes) or the attacker's `ANIM_AO_ACERTAR`.
