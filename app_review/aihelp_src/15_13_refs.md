### 15.13 Where to read next, and what is still unknown (questions 93–97)

**Study order in `TCBManajer.java` [CODE]:** `Run` (29937) → `Game` dispatch (4755) → `CommonNormal` (2747) + `CmdCheck` (2404) → `StartPlayerAttack` (31614) → md 882/883 (21735, 21950) → `PlayerTail` (29714) → `_ActReqMain` (31772) → `CheckOptFlag` (2112) → `CheckObjHit` (2045) → md 935 (23194) → `DamageChange` (3466) → md 876/877 (20983, 21259) → `DamageEnd` (4043) / `AttackEnd` (901) → md 970/971 (24534-24698) → md 996 (25096) → md 19 (15114). The app's `BattleSim` mirrors these (its comments cite `jogo:N`, but **some older citations in the app source point to the wrong lines**; trust the lines in this file).

**Tables available:** this file (15.1–15.12), Appendix A (the app's own help), `GameRules` limits (section 7), HDAT 4 (5.5, 15.2), reactions (5.4, 15.5), flags (5.2, 15.10).

**Open / unconfirmed [UNKNOWN]:** triggers of md 898, 909, 921; meaning of `wOptFlag` 256 and of WACTFLAG 512/1024/2048; HDAT 4 bytes 44/46; whether `tcb.skip` is ever set; md 998–1122; the gamedata/combo-scaling/clash-reaction **values** (in the pacs); the exact units of `AttackMul/DeffenceMul` (percentages inferred); whether the `HOMING == 0` inversion is intended; `ThrowDamage` paths (dead in this build).

**Details easy to miss [CODE]:** the game runs one tick per rendered frame; the same `ano`+`acttype` passed to `_SetAct` is ignored (set `ano = 0` first); a hitbox frame without `$OPT` has damage 0 and reaction 0; OPT1 ≥ 250 and > 10 boxes crash the game; ANO numbers are local to each character's DAC; `-2` speed in reactions is read raw/unsigned; `SetLoad` does not re-read an already loaded pac id.
